The most useful warm-ups are the ones built from the piece you are learning. If the piece is in G major, the G major scale in the rhythm of the hard bar is worth more than any scale chosen at random. This lesson shows how to make exercises from a piece's key, patterns and problems, and the generator writes them out.

:::def Exercise :: a short pattern practised on its own to prepare a skill the piece needs.

## From the key

Start with the scale of the piece, two octaves, slurred and tongued. Then the scale in thirds, which prepares leaps and finger coordination. Then broken triads on each degree, which prepare arpeggio passages and make you hear the harmony. Minor pieces take the natural minor scale and its triads.

## From the rhythm

Take the rhythm of a hard bar and play the scale in that rhythm. Dotted rhythms are a well-tried way to fix uneven semiquavers: play the run as dotted quaver and semiquaver, then reversed, then straight. A passage in triplets takes the scale in triplets.

## From the articulation

Play the scale with the articulation of the passage: slurred in pairs, slurred in fours, two slurred and two tongued, all staccato. Tonguing patterns are a technical challenge in themselves, and the scale is the place to drill them.

## From a specific passage

Isolate the hardest two beats and loop them. Play them with the metronome at half speed, then raise it by a few beats a minute at a time. Play the passage from the end backwards, one beat at a time, so the ending is always secure. Change the rhythm, then change it back.

## Long notes and the drone

For tone and tuning, long notes against a drone are hard to beat. Set the drone in the practice room to the tonic of the piece and play the tonic triad in long notes, listening for the beats that mean you are out of tune and adjusting until they stop. Then do the same with the dominant.

:::aside Quick check :: An exercise is good if you can say which bar of the piece it prepares. If you cannot, it is a general warm-up, which is fine, but keep it short.

:::quiz
